by LICENSES.chromium.html, generated with the original Chromium license scanner.

native/ contains the GLib, zlib and pciutils notices. MODULE.bazel and
upstream_pins.json identify pinned native source dependencies.

contains the complete Debian common license texts referenced by those notices.
